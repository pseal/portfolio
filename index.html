About Me

I am a web development enthusiast who enjoys creating clean, practical, and user-friendly digital experiences. I work mainly with HTML and CSS for front-end development and use SQL and Python for data handling, automation, and improving workflows. Recently expanded my frontend expertise into true full-stack architecture, building secure Python/Django systems backed by relational SQL databases and deploying persistent cloud environments.

I like turning ideas and complex requirements into simple, functional solutions, and I am comfortable working both independently and as part of a team.

Like most modern developers, I incorporate AI tools into my workflow where they make sense — for research, ideation, and catching bugs faster.

Before moving into web development, I spent over 20 years in academic research, working in molecular modeling, chemical kinetics, and reaction mechanisms. That background shaped my problem-solving mindset and attention to detail, which I now bring into my development work.
